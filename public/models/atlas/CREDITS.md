# Biology Atlas — model attribution

This personal educational atlas uses the Human Reference Atlas (HRA) / HuBMAP
v1.2 reference-object library. Downloaded 5 September 2026. The GLB files retain
their original geometry and node names. At runtime they are uniformly scaled,
recoloured by viewing group, and selectively hidden or clipped for exploration.

HRA library: https://humanatlas.io/3d-reference-library
Source release: https://github.com/hubmapconsortium/ccf-releases/tree/main/v1.2/models
Library citation: Kristen Browne, Heidi Schlehlein, Bruce W. Herr II, Ellen
Quardokus, Andreas Bueckle, Katy Börner (2022), HuBMAP CCF 3D Reference Object Library.

## Brain reference — separate Allen Institute terms

`brain.glb` is the original `Allen_M_Brain.glb` from the same HRA release.
It is **not CC BY 4.0**. Allen Institute content is subject to its terms:
https://alleninstitute.org/terms-of-use/

Use here is noncommercial educational exploration. Commercial redistribution
requires Allen Institute permission; do not treat this asset as covered by the
Visible Human models' Creative Commons license.

Citation: Kristen Browne; Heidi Schlehlein (2022), 3D Reference Organ for Brain,
Male v1.2. https://doi.org/10.48539/HBM946.SSFX.636

Underlying reference: Allen Human Reference Atlas — 3D, 2020; Ding et al. (2016).
https://doi.org/10.1002/cne.24080
https://atlas.brain-map.org/

The HRA source mirrors one hemisphere to form the bilateral brain and resizes
it to the Visible Human coordinate frame. It is not a model of one individual's
natural left–right asymmetry. This site's colours group named source regions;
"Deep & medial structures" is a viewing group, not a distinct anatomical system.

## Animal cell

The animal cell is original procedural educational geometry generated by
`src/scripts/biology/cell.ts`. Colours, membrane thickness, chromatin and relative
organelle scales are schematic. The older `models/cell/mitochondria.glb` is retained
but is not loaded by the new atlas.
